Life cycle assessment · BME

EV vs. Petrol Car: Life Cycle Assessment

An ISO 14040/44 cradle-to-use LCA in openLCA with ecoinvent data. It compares a petrol car with an EV charged on the French, German and Hungarian grids.

  • EV cuts lifetime CO₂-eq by 54–78% depending on the grid
  • Climate break-even after about 11,000–16,000 km
  • Interactive explorer for all 18 ReCiPe impact categories

Techno-economics · team project

Bus-to-Grid for Berlin's E-Buses

A techno-economic proposal for adding bidirectional charging at a BVG depot, so parked buses charge cheaply at night and sell power back at peaks.

  • Only +€41M (+6%) CAPEX for bidirectional chargers
  • Benefit in OPEX rises from 16–21% to 22–33%
  • Scenario explorer and Python financial model

Sustainable Aviation Fuel for the Philippines

Sizes the solar or wind capacity, land and investment needed to replace all Philippine jet fuel with e-kerosene made from direct-air-captured CO₂.

  • €4.8–7.9B CAPEX at 2030 technology prices
  • 28–42% more renewables than the 2040 goal
  • Interactive calculator backed by a Python model
